## Privacy Policy for Cardstack App ([one.dav.cardstack](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=one.dav.cardstack))

**Last updated: 24.10.2026**

**Dawid Wąsowski** built the "Cardstack" app as a free app. This SERVICE is
provided by Dawid Wąsowski at no cost and is intended for use as is. This app
is fully offline: it does not collect any data from its users, it does not
require any personal information, and it does not even request internet
access, so it is technically unable to reach the network.

### Information Collection and Use

The "Cardstack" app does not collect any personal data from users.
Furthermore, since no personal data is collected, no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third parties.

Everything stays on your device, inside the app's private storage:

*   your cards (names, codes, photos, notes, reminders),
*   app settings (theme, language, list layout, start screen),
*   the home-screen widget and launcher shortcuts you configure.

Backup files you export (`.cardstack` files) are written only where you
choose to save or share them. Password-protected backups are encrypted on
your device; the password never leaves your device.

### System Permissions

Cardstack requests a minimal set of permissions, each used for one stated
purpose only:

*   **Camera** - only to scan barcodes and photograph cards. The camera is
    active solely while the scanner is open; images are processed on-device.
*   **Notifications** - only to show the expiry reminders you set.
*   **Biometric** - only to unlock the app when you enable the app lock.
    Biometric data never leaves your device's secure hardware and is never
    visible to the app.
*   **Run at startup** - only to re-schedule your reminders after a reboot.

No permission is used for any other purpose, and every feature that needs
one asks first.

### Log Data

The "Cardstack" app does not log any data from users. No usage data, diagnostic
information, or crash reports are sent to the developer.

### Cookies

The "Cardstack" app does not use cookies.

### Service Providers

The "Cardstack" app does not use any third-party services that may collect
information used to identify you. There are no analytics, advertising, or
crash-reporting SDKs included in the app.

### Security

The "Cardstack" app has no servers and stores nothing outside your device.
Uninstalling the app deletes all app data (except backup files you saved
elsewhere).

### Links to Other Sites

The "Cardstack" app links to this policy from its About screen. It contains
no other links to external sites.

### Children's Privacy

The "Cardstack" app does not require any personal information and is safe for
children to use. The app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable
information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

### Data Retention and Deletion

The "Cardstack" app does not collect or retain any personal data on the
developer's side. Data on your device (cards, photos, settings) can be
deleted at any time by removing it within the app, by clearing the app's
data, or by uninstalling the app entirely. Backup files you exported are
deleted by you, wherever you saved them.

### Your Rights

Since the "Cardstack" app does not collect any personal data, there is no data
held by the developer that would be subject to access, correction, or deletion
requests. If you have any privacy-related questions, you are welcome to contact
me at the address below.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review
this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by
adding notes in new releases in Google Play Store.
